    Your key dates in your calendar

    Enter your wedding date: we calculate your deadlines with the Auberge. Then add them to your calendar, with a reminder one week before and the day before. If a deadline has already passed, pick the date when you will complete it.

    Add all to Outlook or AppleCalendar file (.ics) with reminders

      On Google Calendar, add each date with the Google button: reminders follow your Google settings.

      Tick off each step as you go: your checkmarks are saved on this device.
