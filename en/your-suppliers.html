    To share with your suppliers

    Share this post with each of your suppliers.

    • Arrival: tell your wedding coordinator each supplier's arrival time, so our team can welcome them.
    • Equipment: your suppliers check and test their equipment in advance.
    • Departure: they leave the hall no later than 1 hour after the end of the evening and pick up their equipment the next day, no later than 7:30 a.m.
    • Complete list: give your wedding coordinator the names of all your suppliers. Some are not allowed on the premises because of past experiences.

    Cake and flowers

    • The cake and flowers are delivered on the day, or as arranged in advance with your wedding coordinator.
    • Floral arrangements arrive ready: the hall cannot be used to cut flowers or assemble arrangements, otherwise cleaning fees apply.

    DJ, musicians and photo booth

    • Your DJ or musicians work closely with the person in charge of your hall all evening, to synchronize meal service and entertainment. The team leader may adjust service timing as needed.
    • The photo booth is set up in the location designated or approved by your wedding coordinator.
