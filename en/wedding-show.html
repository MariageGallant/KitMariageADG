    The 2027 Wedding Show

    Saturday, January 16, 2027, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Free admission, and your loved ones are welcome to join you.

    We have brought together some twenty suppliers we truly enjoy working with: trusted people who know our venue and know what it takes for a wedding day to run smoothly. They will be set up throughout our reception halls, ready to meet you.

    Why it is worth the trip

    • See our spaces again, to better picture your decor and how the day will unfold
    • Talk with our Wedding Team, on hand to answer your questions
    • Meet our suppliers, all in one day instead of weeks of appointments
    • End the day at the table, at the "Tasting Dinner"
    Add the Wedding Show to Google Calendar Add the Wedding Show to Outlook

    Tasting Dinner

    Once the booths close, Phoenix Hall becomes your wedding reception. Take your seat at a table set just like on the big day, taste our wedding menu and experience the service just as your guests will. It is also the best time to finalize your meal choices.

    • When: Saturday, January 16, 2027, from 4 p.m.
    • Where: Phoenix Hall
    • Who: the couple and up to 2 guests
    • Price: $75 per person, plus taxes and service

    Limited seating: book and confirm your meal choices before December 11, 2026.

    Book your dinner and confirm your meal choices

    Couples attending the dinner also enjoy a preferred rate for their overnight stay.

    Upcoming editions

    Is your wedding in 2028 or later? The Wedding Show returns every year. To find out the date of the next edition and reserve your seat at the Tasting Dinner, ask your wedding coordinator (see "Contact us").
