    Our maple favours

    A sweet souvenir from our maple grove and the Sucrerie des Gallant, to offer your guests.

    Our maple syrup bottles come in four sizes:

    • Medium maple leaf, 100 ml
    • Large maple leaf, 250 ml
    • Mini jug, 100 ml
    • Jug, 250 ml

    Ask your wedding coordinator to place your order.

    Spa Gallant

    The day before the wedding, treat yourself to a moment of relaxation: a massage therapy treatment at Spa Gallant.

    Reservation required for massage therapy treatments: arrange it with your wedding coordinator, no later than the final meeting.

    The outdoor fire pit

    End the evening around the fire, under the stars: offer your guests an outdoor fire.

    • $75, plus taxes and service charges
    • Unlimited firewood
    • Each reception hall has its own designated fire pit
    • You choose the time the fire is lit

    Indicate your choice and preferred time in the "La Grande Finale" form, on the "Wine, bar and late evening" page.
