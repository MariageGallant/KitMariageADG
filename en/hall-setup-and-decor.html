    Hall, setup and decor

    Access, schedule and policies for our reception halls.

    Included with your package

    Cocktail space

    • Tables dressed in white or black linens (low and cocktail tables), chairs

    Reception hall

    • Tables dressed in white or black linens
    • White Chiavari chairs with cushions
    • Table runners, from our 23 colours
    • Gold charger plates, cutlery and glassware
    • White cloth napkins

    After the reception

    • Teardown and regular cleaning
    • Storage of your belongings for easy pick-up the next morning

    Access to your reception hall

    • On the wedding day: from 8 a.m., for decorating and setup.
    • Earlier or the day before: possible depending on our events calendar. The answer is confirmed only during the week before the wedding.
    • End of the reception: your contract includes use of the hall until 1 a.m., at no charge.
    • Extension until 3 a.m.: available only in Phoenix Hall and the Pavillon des Gallant, at $450 per hour, plus taxes and service charges. It can be requested in advance or on the night. The bar option (cash bar, open tab or extended open bar) is to be arranged with your wedding coordinator.
    • Suppliers: they leave the hall no later than 1 hour after the end of the evening.

    The bar after 1 a.m.

    If you extend the evening and would like to keep the bar open after 1 a.m., there are two options:

    • Cash bar: each guest pays for their own drinks.
    • Open tab: you cover your guests' drinks. Only the couple can authorize it, and the amount is settled before you leave the next morning.

    Our table runners

    Your table runners are included with your package. Choose from our 23 colours, in satin, crushed satin or crushed etamine. They are 12 feet long and 6 or 12 inches wide depending on the model.

    Let your wedding coordinator know your choice when planning your reception.

    Your tablecloths, table runners and chair covers

    You may supply your own tablecloths, table runners, napkins or chair covers. Bring them to the Auberge at least 14 days before the wedding: our setup team needs them to prepare the hall. Fees apply if items arrive late.

    Our team sets up your tablecloths, table runners, cloth napkins and charger plates at no charge, since they replace ours. For chair covers, additional setup fees may apply.

    Your place cards, and any other addition to the table setting provided by the Auberge (menus, small gifts, table decor, etc.), are set out by you or your trusted person. Our team does not handle this.

    Decor: what is not permitted

    For everyone's safety and out of respect for the premises, the following are not permitted indoors:

    • Confetti, popcorn, feathers or anything else that requires major cleanup
    • Sparklers and smoke machines (even water-based)
    • Fireworks, unless approved by the Municipality of Sainte-Marthe and the owners

    To hang anything on the walls, beams or ceiling of our reception halls, you need our authorization. Send your wedding coordinator the details of your project in advance: what, where and how it will be attached.

    Failure to follow these guidelines may result in additional fees.

    Candles

    No wax candles or votives directly on the tables. They must be in a candle holder or vase, and the flame must be covered: the holder rises above the wick by at least one third of its height.

    Have your candle holders approved by your wedding coordinator before buying them: she can also suggest alternatives.

    After the reception

    At the end of the evening, our team gathers your items on one table or in one room. Pick them up the next morning, no later than 7:30 a.m.: just like you the day before, the next couple has access to the hall from 8 a.m. After that time, the Auberge is no longer responsible for breakage or theft, unless storage fees are paid.
