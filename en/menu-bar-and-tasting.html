    Our packages at a glance

    Le Classique

    1.5-hour cocktail (sangria or punch, alcohol-free option, 4 canapés per person), 3-course menu, ½ bottle of wine per person, coffee and tea.

    Le Gallant

    1.5-hour cocktail, 4-course menu, ½ bottle of wine per person, 4-hour open bar in the evening, the couple's night in a King or double Queen room, and breakfast.

    Le Gastronomique

    Open bar during the cocktail, sparkling wine toast, 5-course menu, ½ bottle of wine per person, 4-hour open bar in the evening, the couple's night and breakfast.

    Your package details are set out in your contract.

    Your main course choices

    Each package includes three main course choices: two from the menu and one vegetarian option.

    • Additional meal choice: $5 per person, per choice
    • Some dishes carry a supplement depending on the package (filet mignon, lamb shank, etc.)
    • Children's menus available: penne with rosé or tomato sauce, or homemade chicken fingers and fries

    Vegetarians, allergies and dietary restrictions

    All our packages offer a vegetarian or vegan option. In that case, every course, from starter to dessert, is vegetarian.

    We can also accommodate dietary restrictions: gluten-free, lactose-free, shellfish and others. List them in the "La Grande Finale" form.

    The wedding menu tasting

    Before finalizing your choices, come taste our wedding menu, our wines and our service, and make your choices with confidence.

    By appointment

    Book your tasting with your wedding coordinator at least 1 week in advance.

    • Lunch: Thursday, Friday and Saturday
    • Dinner: Thursday and Friday
    • Fees apply
    • Ideal timing: 6 to 8 months before the wedding

    Our Wedding Show and its Tasting Dinner

    Every year, we open our doors to couples-to-be: some twenty of our favourite suppliers, from every field, gathered under one roof at the Auberge. Admission is free.

    The Tasting Dinner

    At the end of the show, Phoenix Hall becomes a wedding reception for a Tasting Dinner: a chance to experience a wedding at the Auberge just as your guests will.

    • Limited seating, reservation required
    • Fees apply

    Next edition

    • Wedding Show: Saturday, January 16, 2027, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
    • Tasting Dinner: from 4 p.m., $75 per person, plus taxes and service

    Ask your wedding coordinator about upcoming dates.

    The open bar

    • Shooters and doubles are not included in any package.
    • Would you like specific or premium drinks? Let your wedding coordinator know in advance.
    • No bottles are placed on the tables: guests do not serve themselves.

    Alcohol: what our permit requires

    Under our liquor permit, it is strictly forbidden to bring in and consume alcohol not purchased on site, including in the parking lots, common areas, gardens and tents.

    • Bottles of alcohol cannot be given to guests as gifts.
    • Tailgating is prohibited; a fine may apply for misconduct or damage.
    • All special orders must go through the Auberge, under certain conditions; additional fees apply.

    Outside food

    It is possible, as long as it is ready to serve: we do not handle food that is not prepared in our kitchens.

    If food needs to be reheated, the chef and your wedding coordinator must approve it at least 3 weeks in advance, and fees apply.

    Your wedding cake

    • Delivered on the day, or as arranged in advance with your wedding coordinator.
    • To serve the cake as dessert, take the cake-cutting photos before the main course is served: plating takes about 45 minutes.
    • Cake cutting and service: free if it replaces our dessert; otherwise, $2.50.
    • Send your serving instructions to your wedding coordinator.

    The late-night buffet

    For one last treat: platters (cheese, charcuterie, sandwiches, mignardises, macarons, pizza), bar-style stations (nachos, ice cream) or individual portions (poutine, smoked meat).

    Base fee of $350, plus a per-person cost based on your selection.

    Lunch while you get ready

    Getting ready at the Auberge? We can serve platters of assorted sandwiches and crudités in your room. Pre-order required, options to discuss with your wedding coordinator.
