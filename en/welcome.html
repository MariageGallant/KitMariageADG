    Congratulations!

    Thank you for choosing us to celebrate your wedding. The whole Auberge des Gallant team is delighted to be part of it.

    This site brings together everything you will need for your preparations: the main milestones, our deadlines, our policies, our tools and our favourite suppliers. Come back to it as often as you like, it is here to make your life easier.

    We look forward to celebrating with you!

    The Auberge des Gallant Wedding Team

    How to use this site

    Each section covers one topic. To find information quickly:

    • Timeline: your key dates with the Auberge, then your preparations period by period, from 18 months before until the day after the wedding. It also comes as an Excel spreadsheet: enter your wedding date and you will know exactly when to plan each step.
    • Topic sections: reception halls, decor, menu, guests, accommodations, suppliers and the wedding day.
    • Downloadable tools: timeline, budget, floor plans, place cards and other templates.

    In the timeline, the ◆ symbol marks a step you complete with the Auberge.

    A few key terms

    Some words you will see throughout this site:

    • Your wedding coordinator: your contact at the Auberge before the big day. She plans with you everything related to our spaces and services.
    • Your banquet manager: on site on the wedding day from 2 p.m., she makes sure the reception runs smoothly and on schedule in our spaces.
    • Your trusted person: someone close to you whom you appoint to coordinate your day outside of our services.
    • Wedding planning service: a professional wedding planning service, independent from the Auberge, that you can hire to coordinate your day.

    Well supported, every step of the way

    A successful wedding relies on careful preparation and clearly defined roles. At the Auberge, our team takes care of everything related to our facilities and services, so that every detail of your reception is ready.

    Before the big day: your wedding coordinator

    She plans with you everything related to our spaces and services: layout, menu, reception schedule and logistics. Everything is ready before you arrive.

    On the wedding day: your banquet manager

    On site from 2 p.m., she is your contact person at the Auberge. She makes sure the reception runs smoothly and follows the planned schedule in our spaces.

    Our advice: your trusted person

    Entrust the coordination of your day to someone close who knows your wishes well, or to a professional wedding planning service. That way, you can fully enjoy every moment.

    What your trusted person looks after

    The Auberge does not offer a wedding planning service or overall coordination of the day. The following items are therefore handled by your trusted person or by the wedding planning service you hire:

    • Travel and transportation
    • Liaising with your outside suppliers
    • Preparations at the church or elsewhere
    • The photo schedule
    • Transitions outside our facilities
    • Unexpected situations and guests' questions

    While your loved ones look after your day, we look after your reception.
