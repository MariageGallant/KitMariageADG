    The wedding day

    How your day unfolds with us.

    On this page

    1. Who does what on the wedding day
    2. Your day, step by step
    3. Breakfast the next morning
    4. Your wedding day kit

    Tick off each step as you go: your checkmarks are saved on this device.

    Who does what on the wedding day

    • Your banquet manager: on site from 2 p.m., she is your contact person at the Auberge and oversees the flow of the reception and the schedule in our spaces.
    • Our service team: follows as closely as possible the flow set with you and your DJ (receiving line, dances, speeches).
    • Your trusted person: transportation, outside suppliers, photos, transitions outside our facilities and unexpected situations.

    Introduce your trusted person to the banquet manager as soon as she arrives.

    Your day, step by step

    • From 8 a.m.: access to your reception hall for decorating.
    • Getting ready: couple's room available from 1 p.m. at the latest; lunch in your room by pre-order; allow at least two hours to get dressed.
    • Ceremony: the bride waits in her room until the wedding coordinator comes to get her.
    • Cocktail: our team directs your guests to the planned space.
    • Reception: place cards set on the tables by you or your trusted person; cake-cutting photos before the main course if the cake is served as dessert.
    • 1 a.m.: end of the reception (until 3 a.m. with an extension, Phoenix and Pavillon only).
    • The day after: breakfast for guests staying at the Auberge, pick-up of your belongings no later than 7:30 a.m. and settlement of the open tab, if any.

    Breakfast the next morning

    Your guests staying at the Auberge start the day with breakfast, served from 8 a.m. to 10 a.m.

    Would guests staying elsewhere like to join you? Note how many in the "La Grande Finale" form, with the planned payment method: at your expense or billed to each guest.
