    Final count and meal choices

    • 1 month before: final guest count and meal choices.
    • From day 21 to day 14: small reductions are possible in case of cancellations.
    • 14 days before: no more substitutions or changes, and no credit.

    Tip: ask your guests to reply at least 6 weeks before the wedding, with their meal choice and allergies.

    Your floor plan

    To hand in 4 weeks before the wedding, at the final meeting:

    • the table layout
    • the number of guests per table
    • the number of each dish, per table
    • the seating of guests with allergies
    • the table numbers
    • any extra tables you need (gifts, cards, candy bar or other)

    The hall is set up only once. Small last-minute changes are accommodated where possible, but nothing is guaranteed within 72 hours of the wedding, and fees may apply.

    Place cards are required

    Place cards allow our team to serve each guest without hesitation and to handle allergies safely. Each one shows:

    • the guest's name
    • their main course choice (beef, chicken, salmon, vegetarian, etc.)
    • the symbol or symbols for their allergies or dietary restrictions, if any

    Set them directly on the tables, at each guest's seat, before guests enter the hall, not on a welcome table. You or your trusted person set them out: our team does not handle this.

    Having them printed? Send our guide to your printer.

    Creating your place cards

    Recommended format

    • Folded tent card, 3.5 x 2 in or 4 x 2.5 in, readable from both sides of the table
    • Single-sided printing is possible, but double-sided is preferred

    Main course choice

    • In words or with an icon. The icon is then solid black, so it is not confused with the allergy and dietary restriction symbols, which are in colour.

    Typography

    • Guest name: minimum 18 pt, ideally 22 to 32 pt
    • Meal choice: minimum 12 pt, ideally 14 to 16 pt
    • An elegant but easy-to-read font

    Colours

    • Light background (white, ivory, cream, very pale pastel) and dark text

    Our guide shows good examples and examples to avoid. Your wedding coordinator may also ask to approve your design.

    Allergy and dietary restriction symbols

    Place the applicable symbols right next to each guest's main course choice.

    Vegetarian · Vegan · Peanuts and tree nuts · Gluten · Shellfish and crustaceans · Eggs · Lactose · Soy · Sulphites · Fish · Other allergy or restriction (to specify)

    • Each symbol is at least 1.25 cm (0.5 in)
    • Its proportions and colours are not altered
    • It is visible at a glance

    To avoid

    • Codes or abbreviations alone (B, P, VG, etc.)
    • Symbols smaller than the minimum size
    • Low-contrast colours
    • Symbols placed far from the meal choice

    In our template, if you choose the "Other allergy or restriction" symbol, a box appears where you can type the details. Keep it short (e.g., Mango, Passion fruit, Halal).

    Guest book, program and speeches

    Think about these about 3 weeks before: the guest book, the guest program and the toast speech. Also appoint someone to bring the important items to the reception, such as the cake knife or toasting glasses.
