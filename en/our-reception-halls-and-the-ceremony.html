    Phoenix Hall

    Grand and contemporary. Large windows onto the forest, cathedral ceiling, private terrace with a tent, large dance floor and bar.

    • Capacity: up to 200 guests
    • Private cocktail space: Phoenix tent
    • Hall dimensions: 60 x 40 feet
    • Reception until 3 a.m. possible, with additional fees

    Vaudreuil-Soulanges Hall

    Elegant and warm. Bright, with a fireplace and private terrace, it suits weddings where closeness matters most.

    • Capacity: 35 to 80 guests
    • Private cocktail space: outdoor gazebo
    • Hall dimensions: 30 x 40 feet
    • Reception ends at 1 a.m. (no extension, as the hall is close to the guest rooms)

    Le Cellier

    Cosy and refined. Nestled beside our wine cellar, it offers an intimate setting for smaller weddings.

    • Capacity: 60 guests or fewer
    • Private cocktail space: outdoor gazebo
    • Hall dimensions: 27 x 30 feet
    • Reception ends at 1 a.m. (no extension, as the hall is close to the guest rooms)

    Pavillon des Gallant

    Rustic, exclusive and countryside. Set apart from the main site, surrounded by a maple grove, with a cathedral roof, large windows and two fireplaces.

    • Capacity: 80 to 125 guests
    • Private cocktail space: Pavillon tent
    • Main hall dimensions: about 31 x 47 feet
    • Ceramic tile area in front of the fireplace, with a slight step
    • Reception until 3 a.m. possible, with additional fees

    Want to hang decor from the beams? See "The Pavillon ceiling".

    The Pavillon ceiling

    Dreaming of hanging decor from the beams of the cathedral ceiling, such as string lights, drapery or greenery? These photos will help you plan. The cathedral ceiling is about 15 ft 11 in wide.

    The chandeliers and string lights are included and cannot be removed: keep this in mind when planning your decor.

    To hang anything on the walls, beams or ceiling of our reception halls, you need our authorization. Send your wedding coordinator the details of your project in advance: what, where and how it will be attached. Wait for our approval before ordering anything.

    The round tables in the second photo were rented by the couple: they are not provided by the Auberge. At the Pavillon, our tables are rectangular.

    The ceremony in our gardens or under the pergola

    Under the willows in our gardens or under the Pavillon pergola, our team prepares the space and takes care of the cleanup.

    Included with your ceremony

    • Chair setup for your guests
    • Signing table with tablecloth
    • Welcome table with fresh water and glassware
    • Natural and potted flowers
    • A table for the DJ and an easel, free of charge on request

    Need extra tables? Let your wedding coordinator know in advance.

    Ceremony music and sound, the officiant and personalized decor are provided by your suppliers.

    Rain or power outage: our backup plans

    If it rains

    Each reception hall has a backup plan prepared in advance, for both the ceremony and the cocktail. After the ceremony, our service team is stationed at key points to direct your guests to the cocktail.

    In case of a power outage

    The Auberge is equipped with generators. If the power goes out, they take over so your reception can carry on.
