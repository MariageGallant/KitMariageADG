    Staying at the Auberge

    Give your guests the comfort of staying on site after the celebration.

    • 42 rooms, King with sofa bed or double Queen
    • Wi-Fi, smart TV, balcony or terrace, air conditioning, electric fireplace, Nespresso machine, bathrobes and towels
    • In the morning: breakfast, access to the thermal baths and heated outdoor pool
    • 4 rooms adapted for people with reduced mobility (Kéroul member)
    • Some rooms welcome pets (conditions apply)
    • 4 electric vehicle charging stations on site, including 1 Tesla charger

    Your room block

    With each of our reception halls, a block of rooms is guaranteed for your guests:

    • Phoenix Hall: 15 rooms
    • Pavillon des Gallant: 10 rooms
    • Vaudreuil-Soulanges Hall: 6 rooms
    • Le Cellier: 4 rooms

    The booking link

    A booking link will be sent to you within the 6 months before your wedding date. Share it with your guests: it gives them access to the preferred rate. Only bookings made through this link receive that rate.

    The rooms in your block are held for your guests until 4 weeks before the wedding. Rooms not booked by then are released automatically.

    The preferred rate

    • $279 per night in 2027 and $289 in 2028, double occupancy, breakfast included
    • Up to 2 additional guests per room: ask your wedding coordinator about the current applicable fees
    • Adding a child to the room: $15
    • Access to our wellness facilities the next morning

    Taxes extra.

    Would your guests rather stay elsewhere?

    There are hotels nearby: you will find their contact details in the "Nearby lodging and transportation" sheet, further down this page.

    The wedding night

    The Le Gallant and Le Gastronomique packages include one night in a King or double Queen room (double occupancy) and breakfast for the couple. With Le Classique, book your room through your wedding coordinator.

    On the wedding day, in your room

    • Couple's room: available from 1 p.m. at the latest.
    • Guest rooms: check-in from 4 p.m. Your guests can also check in during the first minutes of the cocktail, until 6 p.m. at the latest.
    • Before the ceremony, the bride waits in her room until the wedding coordinator comes to get her. Our team makes sure most guests are present before she makes her entrance.
    • Bathrobes are not permitted in the Auberge's common areas.
